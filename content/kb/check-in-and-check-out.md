Applies at all eight properties.

## Check-in time

**Check-in begins at 4:00 PM**, available until 2:30 AM. Settled by Bea on
08/12/26 — the Rules & Regulations (07/22/26) and the homepage were right.

**The FAQ has been corrected and now says 4:00 PM** — re-read live on 08/18/26.
The website and this page finally agree, and 3:00 PM appears nowhere on the site.

It was still there on 08/10/26, so a guest who read the FAQ before mid-August may
quote 3:00 PM in good faith. They are not making it up, and the early arrival fee
below is the one they will be surprised by.

The fees attached to the 4:00 PM window:

- Arriving **before 4:00 PM**: a $25 early arrival fee applies.
- Arriving **after 2:30 AM**: the night is forfeited with no refund.

## Front office hours

Front office hours are 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM and by appointment, every day of the
week, year-round. Move-in is available between 4:00 PM and 2:30 AM.

## Check-out time

**Guests must vacate the room by 11:00 AM** on the scheduled date of departure.
Late departure incurs a $25 fee.

Check-out at 11:00 AM is stated consistently everywhere on the website — there
is no ambiguity about this one.

## Late check-out — and the 1:00 PM cliff

**Late check-out runs to 1:00 PM for a $25 fee. Past 1:00 PM the guest is charged
for an additional day.** (Bea, 08/12/26.)

That boundary is the part worth knowing before a conversation, not after: at
12:55 PM the guest owes $25, and at 1:05 PM they owe a night. It is not on the
website in any form, so a guest cannot have read it, and a GSA who only knows
"$25 late fee" will quote the wrong number to someone already running late.

Early check-in may also be available depending on occupancy. The guest should
call the property ahead to ask. Availability is not guaranteed for either, and
the $25 early arrival / $25 late departure fees are the published charges when it
is granted outside the standard window.

## Digital check-in is required before arrival

All guests must complete the digital or mobile check-in process **prior to
arrival**. It covers:

- photo ID submission
- identity verification (selfie)
- payment confirmation
- acceptance of the house rules

Failure to complete it may result in delayed access or denial of check-in. See
[ID, age, and background checks](/kb/id-age-and-background-checks) for the ID
and age requirements themselves.

## Early departure

No refunds are provided for early check-outs, regardless of reason. Guests are
transient occupants under Florida law.

## Room inspection at check-out

The room is inspected at check-out. Stained linens, smoking in a non-smoking
room, pets in a non-pet room, room damage, and illegal activity are all causes
for deposit forfeiture — see [Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).

Serious violations may place a guest on the Do Not Rent (DNR) list, which is a
permanent ban from all Stayable properties.
