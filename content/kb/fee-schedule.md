Every published fee amount, as stated in the Rules & Regulations document dated
July 22, 2026. Fees not listed here are not published on the website — the pet
fee in particular has no stated amount anywhere.

## All published fees

| Fee | Amount | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Lost key | $2.50 per key | Replacement key |
| Housekeeping (full clean) | $25 | On request, 24 hours notice |
| Linen exchange | $10 | Front desk swap |
| Discarded or damaged linen | $25 | Unrecoverable damage |
| Early arrival | $25 | Before 4:00 PM |
| Late departure | $25 | After 11:00 AM |
| Late payment | $25 | Missed renewal deadline |
| Smoking in a non-smoking room | $250 | Tobacco, vaping, or marijuana |
| Noise violation | $100 | After an initial warning, quiet hours |
| Waste nuisance | $100 | Trash or pet waste outside dumpsters |
| Extra occupancy | $10 per day per person | Beyond 2 persons |
| Card processing | 3% of booking total | Visa, Mastercard, Discover, Amex |
| Window obstruction | $50 per occurrence | Non-Stayable window coverings |
| Car maintenance on property | $100 per occurrence | Vehicle may also be towed |
| Exterior obstruction | $50 per occurrence | Items left outside the room |
| Unattended minor | $100 per occurrence | Unsupervised children on property |
| AC misuse | $25 per occurrence | AC set below 70°F |
| Pool rules violation | $50 per occurrence | Posted pool rule breach |
| Background check | $10 one-time | Extension beyond day 27 |
| WiFi, additional device | $5/day or $50/30 days | Beyond the 3-device free limit |
| Staff residence contact | $100 per occurrence | Approaching staff at home |
| Departure cleaning, 14+ day stay | $50 | Non-refundable |
| Departure cleaning, 28+ day stay | $100 | Non-refundable |

A 7-day notice to vacate may accompany a staff-residence-contact violation.

## Fees with no published amount

- **Pet fee.** Described only as "nominal" or "additional." Confirm with the
  property. Service animals are exempt.
- **Security deposit / damage fee.** The published figures disagree and the
  amount is unconfirmed — see [Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).
- **Room rates.** No nightly, weekly, or monthly rate is published for any
  property, anywhere on the website.
