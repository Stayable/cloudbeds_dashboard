## Two source documents disagree on the replacement key fee

The same workbook lists **"Lost Key Fee — $2.50 per key"** under room fees and
**"Late Fee / Replacement Key Fee — $25"** under payment fees. A replacement key
is therefore $2.50 in one row and $25 in another, in one document, effective the
same day.

**Unresolved — do not pick one at the desk.** $2.50 is the figure the Rules &
Regulations have carried all along and is the safer quote, but the $25 row exists
and somebody wrote it deliberately. Raised 08/18/26.

## Settled internally, still absent from the website

Both amounts above are firm and quotable. What is true of them is not that they
are unknown — it is that **a guest cannot have read them anywhere**, so they will
hear the number from you first, with no published figure to check it against.

- **Pet fee.** Settled by Bea on 08/12/26. The website says only "nominal fee,"
  "additional fee," or "subject to additional fees and charges," and names no
  amount on any page. Service animals and ESAs are exempt — see
  [Pet policy](/kb/pet-policy).
- **Damage fee / deposit.** Settled by Bea on 08/12/26. It varies by **stay
  type**, not by property, which is exactly what the website's "varies by
  location" gets wrong — see
  [Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).

## Genuinely unanswerable from published material

- **Room rates.** No nightly, weekly, or monthly rate is published for any
  property, anywhere on the website, and there is no percentage discount to
  quote either. The live rate in Cloudbeds is the answer — see
  [Discounts and referrals](/kb/discounts-and-referrals).

## Lease residents pay a different schedule

Every fee above is the **transient** schedule. A signed lease has its own — a
different deposit, a different card processing rate, and a late fee that keeps
accruing daily rather than stopping at $25. See
[Lease residents](/kb/lease-residents) before quoting any fee to someone on a
lease.
