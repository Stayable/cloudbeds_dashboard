Every stay includes free WiFi. Devices are tracked by MAC address.

## Free tier

- **Up to 3 devices per unit**, for the entire length of stay.
- Speeds up to **5 Mbps download / 2 Mbps upload**.
- In-room TVs sit on a separate network and **do not count** against the
  3-device limit.

## Paid tier — additional devices

- **$5 per day** or **$50 per 30 days**, per additional device.
- Speeds up to **25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload**.

## Personal network equipment is prohibited

Personal routers, hotspots, MiFi units, and satellite internet are not
permitted on the property. Using cellular data on a guest's own phone is not
prohibited — the restriction is on equipment that creates a competing network.

## Device-limit exceptions

Only Operations or IT may grant a written exception to the device limit, and
only for the current stay. Front desk staff cannot approve one.

## Logging and privacy

Connection logs are retained **for up to 90 days**, solely for legal, security,
and abuse-prevention purposes. Browsing content is not inspected.
