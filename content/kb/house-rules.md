The operational house rules. Fee amounts are cross-listed in
[Fee schedule](/kb/fee-schedule).

## Occupancy limits

- **Two beds:** maximum four guests per unit.
- **One bed:** maximum two guests per unit.
- Extra occupancy beyond two persons: **$10 per day per person**.

## Smoking

- Unless otherwise posted, **all rooms are non-smoking**.
- Smoking is permitted only in designated smoking areas on the ground floor.
- **$250 fine** for smoking in a non-designated area — this covers tobacco,
  vaping, and marijuana alike.
- Smoking in the room is also grounds for full deposit forfeiture.

## Housekeeping

**There is no stayover service and no weekly cleaning service.** Daily cleaning
is not provided at any property. This surprises guests who expect hotel
housekeeping, so set the expectation at check-in.

Available on request, for a fee: a full clean ($25, 24 hours notice) and linen
exchange at the front desk ($10). Discarded or damaged linen is $25.

Guests must keep the room clean and sanitary at all times.

## Air conditioning and smoke detectors

- **Do not put anything on top of the AC unit.**
- Setting the AC below 70°F or otherwise misusing it: **$25 per occurrence**.
- **Do not tamper with or take down the smoke detector for any reason.**

## Trash

Room trash goes to the designated on-site dumpsters only — not in common-area
cans, not left outside the room. **$100 fine.**

## Noise and common areas

- Noise violation after an initial warning during quiet hours: **$100**.
- No running in corridors; no loitering in the lobby, corridors, or walkways.
- Children under 18 should be supervised at all times. An unsupervised child on
  the property is **$100 per occurrence**.

## Pool

- Pool hours are **10:00 AM to 7:00 PM**, weather permitting.
- **Only registered guests are allowed in the pool area.**
- Violating a posted pool rule: **$50 per occurrence**.

Note: the website lists a swimming pool on every property page, **including
Orlando OBT (8700), which does not have a pool.** The website is wrong on this
point. Do not promise a pool at Orlando OBT.

## Parking and vehicles

- Speed limit on property: **5 mph**.
- A valid parking permit must be displayed at all times.
- Vehicles without a permit or with expired registration are towed at the
  guest's expense.
- Only properly licensed and operable motor vehicles. Non-pickup trucks, RVs,
  trailers, boats, and campers are prohibited.
- Leaking vehicles and vehicles under mechanical repair are towed at the
  owner's expense.
- Performing car maintenance on the property: **$100 per occurrence**, and the
  vehicle may be towed.

## Windows, doors, and decorations

- Only Stayable-provided curtains, drapes, and blinds. No films, coatings, or
  hanging decorations. **$50 per occurrence.**
- Door decorations are generally prohibited. One exception: **a single small
  wreath** on a non-damaging over-the-door hanger during the winter holidays.

## Appliances

Only the provided microwave and refrigerator. No crock pots, hot plates, or
similar without written Stayable approval.

## Items outside the room

No personal chairs, plants, or furniture outside the room. If the unit has a
patio or balcony: **one small patio table and two patio chairs** maximum.
Anything else is **$50 per occurrence**.

No personal BBQ grills — only Stayable-provided grills.

## Bicycles and scooters

No riding bicycles, scooters, or skateboards on the property. Bikes belong in
the designated racks or they may be removed.

## Weapons

Firearms and other deadly weapons are not permitted on the property.

## Contacting staff at home

**Under no circumstances may a guest knock on, approach, or otherwise attempt
to contact a manager or staff member at their personal residence.** $100 per
occurrence, and it may carry a 7-day notice to vacate.

## Unit access by management

Management and maintenance may enter at reasonable times, with reasonable
notice, except in an emergency.

## Video surveillance

Common areas — lobby, pool, laundry, and exterior — carry video and audio
surveillance. **Guest rooms are exempt.** Entering a common area constitutes
consent to being recorded. Footage is used solely for security, with access
limited to authorised personnel.
