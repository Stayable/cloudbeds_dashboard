## No credit check

**No credit check is required to stay at Stayable.** This is a deliberate
policy, stated plainly on the FAQ: "we skip the credit check entirely." A valid
ID and same-day move-in on a weekly or monthly term is the intended path.

A credit check is not the same thing as the ID verification and background
check described below — those are still required. If a guest asks "do you run
my credit," the answer is no.

## Age requirement

- **21 or older to check in**, with a valid government-issued photo ID.
- Guests **18 or older** who are not the person checking in must still register
  with a valid photo ID.
- The website's Terms & Conditions separately require users to be 18+ to use
  the website or make a reservation. That is a website term, not the check-in
  age. The check-in age is 21.

The 21+ requirement appears only in the Rules & Regulations — it is not on the
FAQ or homepage, so a guest may arrive not knowing it.

## Photo ID

All guests must be registered with a valid photo ID, which remains on file.
Identity verification is required before check-in.

## Digital check-in

Digital or mobile check-in must be completed before arrival: photo ID
submission, a selfie for identity verification, payment confirmation, and
acceptance of the house rules. Failure to complete it may cause delayed access
or denial of check-in.

## Guest screening (Autohost)

Identity verification and risk assessment run through a third-party partner,
**Autohost**. Failing screening may result in cancellation of the reservation
without a refund.

## Background check for stays beyond 27 days

Guests extending a stay **beyond 27 consecutive days** must complete a criminal
background check. **One-time $10 fee.**

- **Scope:** limited — it identifies recent, serious criminal charges or
  convictions involving violence, drugs, theft, or serious sex offenses. It is
  not a general credit or employment check.
- **Failing or not completing it** may mean the extension is not approved
  beyond day 27.
- **Exemption:** guests who book and pay in full for 28 days or longer *at the
  time of the initial reservation* are exempt — but only for that original
  stay. Any extension beyond the original paid stay requires a background check.

That exemption is the detail most often missed: paying for 28 days up front
avoids the check now, not forever.
