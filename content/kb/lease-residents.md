A signed lease is a **different product on the same property**, not a long
transient stay. Almost every number a guest asks about forks here, and quoting
the transient figure to a lease resident is the most common way to be wrong
while sounding certain.

## The fork, in one table

| Question | Transient | Signed lease |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum age to check in | 21 | **18** |
| Deposit | $100 per room | **Starts at $250** per unit — see the caveat below |
| Pet fee | $15/day or $50/week, no pet deposit | **$25 per pet, per month**, plus a $250 pet deposit per unit |
| Housekeeping | On request for a fee ($25 full clean, $10 linen) | **Not available.** Not included and not offered |
| Card processing | 3% of the booking total | **3.49%**, charged through TurboTenant |
| Late payment | $25 | **$25 on day one, then $10 every further day** |
| Monthly rate | Not available | This is the only way to get one |
| Extra occupancy basis | Beyond 2 persons | Beyond the number of tenants **named on the lease** |

Nothing in this table is on rentstayable.com. The lease track is essentially
undocumented publicly, so a lease applicant arrives knowing none of it.

## The deposit is a floor, not a flat figure

Bea gave **$250 per unit** on 08/12/26. The Leasing Fee Schedule words it
differently: **"Starts at $250 — refundable deposit collected at lease signing;
may be set higher based on tenant screening or unit."**

**Say "starts at $250," not "$250."** The two are not in conflict about the
number, but they are in conflict about whether it is fixed, and an applicant
told a flat $250 who is then quoted more at signing has been misled by us.
Confirm the actual figure with the property before it is put in writing.

## Late rent accrues daily — this is the one people get wrong

- **$25** one-time, assessed the first day rent is late.
- **$10 per day** from the second day onward, accruing until rent is paid in
  full.

The transient schedule stops at $25 and does not accrue. A resident four days
late owes $25 + $30, not $25.

## Rent is paid through TurboTenant

Card payments on a lease run through **TurboTenant at 3.49%**, not the 3%
transient card processing fee. Different system, different rate.

## House rules for lease residents

From the *Lease Residents – House Rules & Guidelines* document. Where a rule is
identical to the transient rule it is not repeated here — see
[House rules](/kb/house-rules) for smoking, parking, pool, decorations, and the
rest, all of which apply unchanged.

**General**

- All lease terms, policies, and property rules stay in effect for the whole
  tenancy.
- Non-compliance can mean fees, a lease violation, or termination — a harder
  consequence than the transient equivalent, which is removal without refund.

**Occupancy**

- **Only registered occupants may reside in the unit.** The transient bed-count
  limits (4 with two beds, 2 with one bed) are not the operative rule here — the
  lease names who lives there.
- Guests of a resident must comply with all property rules.

**The unit itself**

- Keep it clean and sanitary; dispose of trash in the designated dumpsters.
- Keep appliances and fixtures in good condition.
- Do not tamper with smoke detectors or misuse the AC — the **$25 AC misuse fee**
  applies on a lease exactly as it does transient.

**Maintenance and access**

- Work orders go through the proper channels, not to staff directly.
- Management may enter with notice for inspection or maintenance.

**Decorations** — the same policy as transient, stated more explicitly: nothing
taped, glued, nailed, or attached to unit doors or windows at any time,
including holiday décor, posters, banners, streamers, and adhesives. One
exception, the winter holidays: **a single small wreath on a non-damaging
over-the-door hanger.** Non-compliant items must come down on notice and
management may remove them.

**Pets** — must be approved and comply with the pet policy, supervised and
cleaned up after at all times. See [Pet policy](/kb/pet-policy) for the fees,
which differ from transient.

## What is NOT settled for lease residents

- **Service animal and ESA documentation.** A lease is housing and a transient
  stay is a public accommodation; they are governed differently and one desk
  rule cannot cover both. Referred for legal review 08/13/26 — see
  [Pet policy](/kb/pet-policy). Do not decide this at the desk.
- **The per-property damage fee** is now **partly settled on the leasing
  schedule** and still open on the transient one — the 09/04/26 edit to the fee
  workbook broke them apart. Kissimmee East (2295) leasing is **$1,300** and
  quotable. Kissimmee West (5399) leasing shows **$900** but the same row still
  says "confirm", so it is not quotable. Jacksonville North (812) has nothing on
  either sheet. See [Fee schedule](/kb/fee-schedule).
- **Whether the deposit varies by property as well as by screening.** The
  workbook says it may be set higher "based on tenant screening or unit," which
  does not say which.
