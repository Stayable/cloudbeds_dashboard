## Package pickup hours

Mail and packages are collected at the front desk during these windows only.

| Days | Windows |
|---|---|
| Monday to Friday | 9:00 AM – 9:30 AM · 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM · 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM |
| Saturday and Sunday | 1:00 PM – 3:00 PM · 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM |

## Staff will not confirm receipt

**Staff will not confirm receipt of mail or packages** by phone or text. The
guest must come to the desk during a pickup window. This is policy, not
discretion — do not check "just this once," because it becomes the expectation.

## ID required

A valid photo ID is required to pick up a package.

## Long-term mail

A blog article on the website suggests guests with long stays may prefer a PO
box or a private mailbox service for stability. That is general advice offered
to guests, not a Stayable service — the pickup windows above are the actual
operative policy.
