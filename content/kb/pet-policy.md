All Stayable locations are pet-friendly. Pets are permitted only when expressly
authorised in writing by Stayable.

## What pets are allowed

- **Dogs under 35 pounds.** No aggressive breeds. Some breed restrictions apply.
- **Cats.**
- **Other pets** such as birds must be kept caged and are subject to management
  approval.
- **No more than two pets per unit.**

## Pet fee

A pet fee applies. **The website never states a dollar amount** — every page
says "nominal fee," "additional fee," or "subject to additional fees and
charges" without a number. Confirm the current amount with the property before
quoting it to a guest.

**Service animals are exempt from all additional fees and charges.**

## Rules while on the property

- All dogs must be on a leash in all common areas at all times.
- Guests must clean up after their dogs at all times. Leaving pet waste on the
  grounds triggers the **$100 waste nuisance fee**.
- All pets on the premises must be vaccinated and licensed.
- Pets may not be left outdoors unsupervised.
- Pets must be kept in appropriate, contained areas within the room.
- Pets must be crated or otherwise contained when left unattended in the unit.

## Violations

Violations of the pet policy may be grounds for immediate removal from the
property without a refund. A pet found in a non-pet room is also a cause for
deposit forfeiture at check-out — see
[Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).

Stayable may change the pet policy at any time at its sole discretion.
