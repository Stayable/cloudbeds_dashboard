Reference for questions guests raise about their data and the fine print. This
is a summary of published terms, not legal advice — route anything contested to
`legal@rentstayable.com`.

## What data is collected

- Name, postal address, email, phone, and credit card information.
- IP address, browser and OS, and usage patterns via cookies and web beacons.
- Screening data — government ID, selfie / biometric data, and background
  checks. The policy attributes this to **Autohost**, which is out of date; see
  the note below.

## Who it is shared with

- Service providers and contractors
- Autohost (screening) — **as written in the published policy; see below**
- A buyer or successor in a merger, divestiture, or restructuring
- Law enforcement or government on legal request
- Fraud and credit-risk partners

**The published policy still names Autohost, and screening moved to AKIA**
(Bea, 08/12/26; confirmed by Kyle 08/13/26). Both mentions above are recorded as
the website words them, because that is what a guest will quote back — but do not
tell a guest that Autohost handles their data today. The Terms & Conditions carry
the same stale name, still live on 08/18/26. See
[ID, age, and background checks](/kb/id-age-and-background-checks).

**Retention period is not published.** The privacy policy says only that
information is used for communication related to the guest's lease application
or requested services. If a guest asks how long their data is kept, that is not
answerable from the website.

## Marketing consent and opting out

Submitting information on the website consents to telemarketing messages by
email, landline, cellular, SMS, and MMS — including pre-recorded voice messages
and automatic dialing — **even if the number is on a Do-Not-Call list**.

To opt out: reply **STOP** to an SMS, use the unsubscribe link in an email, or
write to `legal@rentstayable.com`. Processing can take **up to 30 days**.

## Dispute resolution

- **Mandatory binding arbitration** under AAA rules.
- **Class action waiver** — no class actions, and no consolidating claims with
  other people's.
- Governing law: **Florida**. Venue: **Palm Beach County**.
- **One-year statute of limitations.**
- The prevailing party recovers costs and reasonable attorneys' fees through
  trial and appeal.

## Website terms

Use of the website is at the user's own risk. The company disclaims warranties
and accepts no liability for DDoS attacks, viruses, malware, or technical
failures. Users must be 18 or older to use the site or make a reservation —
which is separate from the **21+ check-in age**, covered in
[ID, age, and background checks](/kb/id-age-and-background-checks).

## Surveillance

Common areas carry video and audio surveillance; guest rooms do not. See
[House rules](/kb/house-rules).
