## The reservation is only as long as it is paid

"Your reservation is valid only for the time that is fully paid on your folio."
There is no grace period built into the booking itself.

## Renewal deadline

- Renewal payment is due by **11:00 AM on the renewal day**.
- Non-payment forfeits the room.
- Late payment is a **$25 fee** plus the room charge.
- Extensions are subject to availability.

## Length of stay

There is no maximum stay. Stayable offers weekly and monthly terms with no
long-term commitment and no 12-month lease requirement; guests renew on a
rolling basis, and many stay for several months or longer.

Two thresholds change what is required, and they are easy to miss:

- **Beyond 27 consecutive days** — a $10 criminal background check is required
  before the extension is approved. See
  [ID, age, and background checks](/kb/id-age-and-background-checks).
- **30+ consecutive days** — may change lodging tax treatment by state and
  county. The website does not name a specific tax or rate and explicitly
  disclaims tax advice. Do not quote a tax outcome to a guest.

Departure cleaning fees also step up at 14 and 28 days — see
[Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).

## Card payments

- Card processing fee: **3% of the booking total** (Visa, Mastercard, Discover,
  American Express).
- The guest authorises Stayable to charge the card on file for any outstanding
  amount on the account. A signed final invoice at check-out is not required.

## No refunds for early departure

No refunds are provided for early check-outs, regardless of reason. Guests are
transient occupants under Florida law.

## Cancellation

**There is no published cancellation window.** The Terms & Conditions state
only that Stayable may terminate the agreement at any time, with or without
notice — that is a Stayable-side right, not a guest-facing cancellation policy.
If a guest asks about cancelling, this is not answerable from published
material; route it to the property.
