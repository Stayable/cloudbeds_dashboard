Apartment-style hotel suites with kitchenettes and living areas.

## What is in every suite

- Furnished bedroom — bed, fresh linens, pillows, nightstand
- Kitchenette with **refrigerator and microwave**
- Living area with TV and seating
- Table and chairs (many suites)
- Complimentary high-speed WiFi — see [Guest WiFi](/kb/guest-wifi)
- **All utilities included in the rate**

Bed configurations are king or double. Most property pages describe a **King
Room** and a **Double Room** (two full-size beds), each with a workstation and
a smart TV with streaming.

## Property-level amenities

- **Free parking** at every location.
- **On-site laundry at select properties** — not all. Confirm before promising it.
- Housekeeping and linen exchange available for a fee. There is no included
  daily or weekly cleaning; see [House rules](/kb/house-rules).
- Pet-friendly at all locations — see [Pet policy](/kb/pet-policy).

## Swimming pool — unverified, do not promise one

The website says a swimming pool is available "at every location," and lists one
on all eight property pages.

**There is reason to believe at least one of those pages is wrong — Orlando OBT
(8700) in particular — but it has not been verified.** All eight properties have
been asked to confirm on the Property Information Request.

Until they answer: **do not promise a pool at any property.** Check with the
property. A guest who was promised a pool that does not exist is a complaint the
website caused and we repeated.

## Property-specific differences

**Kissimmee West (5399)** is the outlier. Its page lists two amenities that
appear nowhere else — a **hot tub** and a **children's play area** — and its
room types are **King Room and Queen Room** (two queen beds), not the King /
Double pattern used elsewhere.

**Lakeland (4645)** states explicitly that a refundable damage fee applies per
room per stay.

**Jacksonville North (812)** and **Jacksonville West (6802)** both advertise
the 5% military and first-responder discount and the referral program on their
own pages — see [Discounts and referrals](/kb/discounts-and-referrals).

## What the website does not state

- **No room rate** — nightly, weekly, or monthly — for any property.
- **No square footage** for any room or suite type.

Neither figure exists anywhere on the public site, so neither can be quoted
from it.
