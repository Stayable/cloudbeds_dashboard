A deliberate list of questions this knowledgebase **cannot** answer from
published material. It exists so that a search which finds nothing useful still
tells you *why* — an unanswered question is better than a confidently wrong
answer invented to fill the gap.

If you resolve one of these, it should move into the relevant document and come
off this list.

## Not published anywhere on the website

- **Any room rate**, nightly, weekly, or monthly, for any property.
- **Square footage** of any room or suite type.
- **The exact pet fee** — described only as "nominal" or "additional."
- **The after-hours emergency phone number** — referred to but never given.
- **An explicit cancellation window** for guests. The Terms & Conditions only
  give Stayable a unilateral right to terminate.
- **A data retention period** in the privacy policy.
- **Open job postings or how to apply** — the careers page has categories and
  culture copy only, and names no HR contact beyond the general contact page.
- **A procedure for requesting a consolidated invoice** for an insurance or ALE
  claim. A blog article implies one can be produced but names no process or
  contact.

## Open internally, not just on the website

- **The security deposit amount.** The Deposit Terms page says a flat $100; the
  homepage and FAQ say it varies by location. Being confirmed with the team as
  of 08/12/26 — see [Deposit and damage fee](/kb/deposit-and-damage-fee).

## Known to be wrong on the website

- **Orlando OBT (8700) is listed with a swimming pool. It has none.**
- **The FAQ says check-in is 3:00 PM. It is 4:00 PM.**
- **The contact page lists `lakeland@rentstayable.com` for Lakeland.** Use
  `frontdesk@rentstayable.com`.

## Uncertain

- Whether **on-site laundry, the hot tub, and the children's play area** are
  offered portfolio-wide or are specific to Kissimmee West. The site copy
  suggests they are Kissimmee-West-specific, but only that property's page
  lists them, which is weak evidence either way.
